# Project Status

**Status date:** 18 September 2026
**Current stage:** Research design and corpus-readiness  
**Immediate milestone:** Freeze the SAP release and process scope, then complete the pilot corpus and codebook pilot.

## Overall assessment

The project has a coherent conceptual, methodological, and manuscript architecture for studying enterprise software as codified organisation design. The research environment is ready to support controlled empirical collection and pilot coding. It is not yet an empirical study: no SAP artefacts have been registered or coded, and no findings have been established.

## Completed

### Research design and theory

- The primary question and five supporting research questions are defined.
- SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition is established as the instrumental case, with P2P, O2C, and R2R as the intended process comparison.
- The conceptual framework distinguishes encoded organisational logic, implemented organisational configuration, and enacted organisational practice.
- Thompson, Galbraith, and Mintzberg provide the main theoretical lenses.
- Software organisational primitives are specified as a candidate contribution rather than an established result.

### Method and analytical infrastructure

- The research and data-collection protocols define source hierarchy, inclusion boundaries, release control, capture rules, and evidence provenance.
- The two-stage coding manual separates descriptive software observations from theoretical interpretation.
- The analytical framework defines within-process, cross-process, multilevel, rival-interpretation, and negative-case analysis.
- Corpus-register, evidence-log, coding-matrix, literature-matrix, and memo templates are available.
- Figure/table planning, risk controls, a paper pipeline, and a twelve-week execution plan are documented.

### Literature and manuscript development

- A working literature map, literature review, reading route, and bibliography are in place.
- The organisation-design stream now has a detailed review and a source assessment that distinguishes core sources, adjacent sources, exclusions, and important omissions.
- The coordination-theory stream now has a detailed review and source audit. It distinguishes dependencies, mechanisms, artefacts, integrative conditions, and outcomes; records full-text versus abstract/metadata access; and rejects prompt citations and unresolved database links as evidence.
- A second LeapSpace coordination report has been audited and incorporated selectively. Four ERP-specific sources were retained with explicit access and scope limits; prompt citations, opaque uploads, mismatched levels of analysis, and the report’s unsupported confidence rating were rejected.
- The decision-theory stream now has a detailed review and source assessment. It distinguishes decision premises, programmes, authority, permission, discretion, automation, traceability, and accountability, while rejecting opaque uploads, prompt citations, and irrelevant retrievals from the supplied LeapSpace report.
- A SciSpace Decision Theory review and its citation audit have been assessed. Five sources were added selectively to strengthen decision premises, organisation-structure links, institutional effects, formal versus real authority, and application-level decision rights. Unsupported SAP examples and overbroad automation propositions were rejected.
- All three completed theory streams now use a three-layer reading structure: a learning guide, a readable scholarly synthesis, and a complete technical expansion. The technical expansions have also received a paragraph-level readability revision, with problem-led openings, definitions at first use, explicit connections among authors, shorter argument stages, and labelled illustrations. A project-wide scholarly writing and teaching standard governs the consolidated review and book so that accessibility improvements retain theoretical detail, evidence boundaries, propositions, and citation coverage.
- Working drafts exist for the abstract, introduction, theory, method, and conclusion.
- Findings and discussion files are deliberately conditional structures, not statements of empirical results.
- The repository README now documents the project objectives, scope, deliverables, workflow, and research-quality principles.
- A SAP Learning workspace now consolidates the current End-to-End Business Processes and SAP Activate notes, records linked sources and access limitations, and maps certification material to a controlled empirical data-collection route.
- A separate book workspace now defines the proposed Springer research monograph, provisional chapter architecture, audience and market position, initial competing-title assessment, reuse controls, and evidence-based proposal gate.
- A dedicated course workspace now defines a postgraduate master course, twelve programme learning outcomes, a twelve-week curriculum, an aligned assessment strategy, ten module scaffolds, teaching and evidence controls, content templates, and governed undergraduate and executive pathways. No module or SAP case is yet represented as teaching-ready.
- The authoritative literature map now contains nine non-duplicative streams. The former enterprise-systems stream is expanded to implementation and transformation, the governance stream is expanded to accountability and decision rights, and business-process management and process architecture is added as Stream 9. The course crosswalk links every module to this shared structure and separates substantive scholarship, SAP case evidence, and course-only teaching-design literature.

## Empirical work not started

- The target SAP release remains unfrozen.
- Exact official solution-process IDs and inclusion boundaries for P2P, O2C, and R2R have not been recorded.
- The corpus register, evidence log, and coding matrix contain schemas only; they contain no empirical records.
- No SAP reference artefacts have been captured, archived, segmented, or triangulated.
- The codebook has not been piloted or released as version 1.0.
- No within-process mechanism maps, cross-process comparisons, negative-case results, or confidence judgements exist.
- Manuscript corpus counts and evidence counts remain explicit placeholders.
- No empirical figures or tables have been produced.

## Claims that must not yet be made

Do not claim that SAP improves coordination, produces centralisation or standardisation in adopting organisations, determines roles or practices, ensures compliance, or yields performance outcomes. Do not claim that candidate primitives are validated, that Fit-to-Standard is empirically a negotiation, that configuration constitutes organisational discretion in every case, or that patterns generalise beyond SAP. Vendor documentation can support claims about encoded possibilities and constraints, not implemented arrangements or enacted practice.

## Next five research actions

1. Freeze the target SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition release and enumerate the official P2P, O2C, and R2R solution-process IDs with explicit inclusion and exclusion boundaries.
2. Complete the core bibliography and literature-matrix verification for Thompson, Galbraith, Mintzberg, and the principal organisation-design sources.
3. Build a pilot corpus containing the structural/IAM materials and at least two complementary official artefacts for each process domain, using the corpus register and immutable capture rules.
4. Pilot descriptive and theoretical coding, document ambiguities and disagreements, revise the manual, and release codebook version 1.0.
5. Produce one evidence-backed dependency/mechanism map per process and an initial claim–evidence–rival table before drafting empirical findings.

Book-development work should remain secondary to these five actions until the empirical pilot is complete. After that gate, reassess the chapter architecture, prepare one sample chapter, and develop a submission-ready Springer proposal.

Course development may proceed in parallel where it repackages already verified theory. Empirical SAP teaching cases remain gated by the same release freeze, corpus registration, and evidence controls as the research.

## Next decision gate
