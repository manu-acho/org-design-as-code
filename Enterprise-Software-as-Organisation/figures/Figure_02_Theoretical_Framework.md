# Figure 2. Theoretical framework

![Differentiated activities create dependencies. Coordination, information processing, and decision and governance are complementary lenses on the operation that connects, informs, assigns or constrains work. They do not establish effective coordination or real authority.](Figure_02_Theoretical_Framework.svg)

**Caption.** The framework starts with a dependency between differentiated activities, not a list of software features. Its three lenses ask different questions of the same operation; they are not successive stages. A documented consequence supports a claim about the represented arrangement, not its effects in practice.

**Provenance.** Original conceptual synthesis for Paper 1, 10 November 2026. See the [paper's theoretical framework](../paper/03_Theory_Draft.md) and [consolidated review](../04_Literature_Review_Working_Draft.md) for definitions, citations and scope.
