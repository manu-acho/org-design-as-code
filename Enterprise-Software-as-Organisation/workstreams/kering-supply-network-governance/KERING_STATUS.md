## Decision gates

### Gate 1: Feasible documentary case

Proceed only if the public corpus supports a bounded unit of analysis, multiple relevant artefact classes, reliable provenance, and claims more specific than a summary of corporate policy.

### Gate 2: Architectural-coherence pilot

Proceed to full documentary collection only if a small pilot can reconstruct at least one governance interface with traceable actors, information, rights or controls, exceptions, and rival interpretations.

### Gate 3: Implementation-fit study

Proceed only if access provides direct evidence of actual system, process, role, contract, or integration choices and the necessary ethics and confidentiality controls are in place.

### Gate 4: Enacted-fit study

Proceed only if independent and ethically safe access to relevant supplier-side or worker-side participants is possible. Corporate interviews alone do not satisfy this gate.

## Priority relative to the SAP study

The SAP architectural-coherence pilot remains the programme's first empirical priority. Kering feasibility work may proceed in a bounded way, but it must not delay the release freeze, pilot corpus, codebook validation, or initial SAP mechanism maps.
