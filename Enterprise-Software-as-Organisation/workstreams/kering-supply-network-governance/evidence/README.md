# Evidence Protocol

## Purpose

This protocol governs the Kering workstream only. Its corpus must remain separate from the SAP reference-architecture corpus because the cases have different sources, versions, units of analysis, and inferential limits.

## Source hierarchy

### Grade A: Authoritative primary sources

- applicable legal texts and official consolidated versions;
- official Kering registration documents and regulated disclosures;
- dated Kering policies, standards, codes, charters, and governance documents;
- official portals and system documentation that can be captured with date and version;
- contracts, procedures, audit instruments, and implementation records obtained with permission;
- direct interviews and field observations collected under an approved protocol.

### Grade B: Strong contextual sources

- official regulator and public-authority guidance;
- official industry-association records;
- peer-reviewed empirical studies of the relevant industrial districts, supply networks, or governance mechanisms;
- independently documented case material with transparent methods.

### Grade C: Discovery and triangulation sources

- reputable journalism;
- consultancy and technology-provider reports;
- corporate communications without sufficient operational detail;
- conference presentations and public commentary.

Grade C material may identify leads or contested claims. It cannot independently establish internal arrangements or outcomes.

## Initial official-source anchors

The feasibility audit should begin with these currently identified official sources and then capture exact versions:

- Kering Universal Registration Document and Duty of Care Plan;
- Kering Sustainability Library and ESG disclosures;
- Kering Standards and supplier-facing charters;
- Kering Vendor Collaboration Area and publicly visible system descriptions;
- relevant Kering statements about supplier governance, craftsmanship, industrial organisation, and strategic partnerships;
- the applicable consolidated text of Directive (EU) 2024/1760 and related amendments;
- the applicable CSRD legislation, European Sustainability Reporting Standards, and amendments;
- relevant Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation and digital product passport materials if they fall within the frozen scope.

This list is a collection route, not evidence that every item is relevant or sufficient.

## Regulatory discipline

Do not combine reporting, due diligence, product-information, and national duty-of-care obligations into a single claim of full supply-chain accountability. For every legal claim, record:

- instrument and article or authoritative section;
- version and consolidation date;
- territorial and organisational scope;
- application or transposition date;
- obligation type;
- entity to which the obligation applies;
- whether the statement concerns law, guidance, corporate policy, or anticipated change.

## Collection workflow

1. Freeze the case, process, time, and regulatory boundaries.
2. Register the source before extracting evidence.
3. Preserve a dated copy where legally and technically permitted.
4. Record the exact page, section, screen, or field location.
5. Describe the represented feature before applying theory.
6. Separate corporate assertion from independently established fact.
7. Record rival interpretations and missing evidence.
8. Assign a confidence judgement proportionate to the source set.

## Evidence classes

Each extracted segment must be tagged as one of the following:

- legal requirement;
- corporate policy or standard;
- represented system capability;
- represented process or role;
- corporate report of activity or outcome;
- independent observation;
- participant account;
- researcher interpretation.

These classes must not be collapsed during synthesis.

## Technology claim controls

Do not infer operational use of ERP, PLM, blockchain, artificial intelligence, digital twins, or digital product passports from general corporate strategy or industry discussion. A technology claim requires a source that identifies the system or capability, its organisational scope, its function, and the relevant date.

## Current verified discovery points

As of 19 October 2026, official public pages indicate that Kering publishes registration and sustainability material, operates supplier-facing information and control interfaces, and publicly discusses supplier scorecards, audited standards, craftsmanship, traceability, and a strategic joint venture with HModa. These discovery points justify a feasibility audit. They do not establish implementation quality or enacted effects.
