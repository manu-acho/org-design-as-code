# Proposal Intake and Assessment

## Source status

The workstream originated in the supplied document **Case Study Proposal: Upstream Supply Chain Governance & Artisan Ecosystem Integration at Kering**.

The proposal is a research brief and discovery input. It is not empirical evidence about Kering, its Houses, suppliers, subcontractors, workers, systems, or outcomes. Its factual claims and candidate frameworks must be verified independently before use in a scholarly output.

The original PDF remains outside the research repository. It has not been copied into the workstream or treated as part of the empirical corpus.

## Retained research opportunity

The proposal identifies a potentially valuable tension between industrial capacity and integration on one side, and decentralised artisan flexibility on the other. It also raises questions about multi-tier governance, digital traceability, supplier autonomy, labour standards, and compliance across organisational boundaries.

These topics fit the programme when reformulated as questions about represented and enacted governance arrangements rather than as assumed facts about a named company.

## Required corrections before empirical use

1. Separate legal reporting duties, due-diligence duties, product-information requirements, corporate policy, and voluntary ambition.
2. Define the unit of analysis and prevent silent movement among Group, House, supplier, workshop, worker, product, and district levels.
3. Verify every Kering-specific assertion against dated primary evidence and, where appropriate, independent evidence.
4. Treat ERP, PLM, blockchain, digital product passports, and other technologies as possibilities until operational use and scope are documented.
5. Replace claims of a literature gap with a reproducible search and source assessment.
6. Separate architectural coherence, implementation fit, and enacted fit according to the evidence each requires.
7. Establish whether the intended study is explanatory, comparative, evaluative, or primarily reconstructive.

## Retained candidate theories

The proposal suggests global value-chain governance, agency theory, transaction-cost economics, and dynamic capabilities. These remain candidate contextual lenses. Inclusion depends on source verification, conceptual necessity, and demonstrated contribution beyond the shared nine streams.

## Intake decision

**Decision:** retain as the initiating concept for a separate empirical workstream.

**Conditions:** do not merge it with the SAP corpus; do not create a tenth foundational literature stream; do not make Kering-specific or legal claims from the proposal alone; and do not move beyond documentary architectural analysis without the evidence and ethics gates defined in this workstream.
