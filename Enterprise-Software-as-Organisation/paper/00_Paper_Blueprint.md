# Paper Blueprint

The [working manuscript](README.md) now applies this blueprint. Its scope is the three bounded Swiss reference cases, not a claim about customer systems or outcomes.

**Working title:** *Enterprise Software as Codified Organisation Design: Reconstructing Coordination and Governance in SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition*

**Research question:** How are organisational coordination and governance mechanisms encoded within enterprise software reference architectures?

**Contribution statement:** The paper develops and applies an architectural-documentary method for reconstructing encoded organisational logic. It tests whether recurring software organisational primitives instantiate configurations of interdependence, coordination, information processing, governance, control, and discretion. It does not claim organisational effects.

**Gap:** Enterprise-systems studies richly explain implementation and use, while organisation-design theories explain coordination and information processing. The reference architecture as an organisational artefact remains comparatively under-specified as a primary object.

**Case and method:** Instrumental embedded case of SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition; release-controlled official corpus spanning P2P, O2C, R2R and cross-cutting structure/IAM; two-stage coding, within-process mechanism mapping, cross-process pattern matching, negative cases and rivals.

**Expected architecture and word budget:** Introduction 1,500; theory 3,000; method 2,000; findings 3,500; discussion 2,500; conclusion 600: approximately 13,100 words before journal adjustment. Empirical and conceptual contributions remain conditional on evidence.
