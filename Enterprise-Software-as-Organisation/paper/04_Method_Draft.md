# Method  -  Working Draft

## Research design and case selection

The study uses an embedded instrumental case design to examine SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition. SAP is selected for the breadth and explicitness of its official reference content across enterprise structure, solution processes, roles, authorisations, applications, configuration, and extensibility. Public Edition makes standard content and sanctioned variation especially visible. Selection is theoretical, not a claim of representativeness.

## Corpus construction

The corpus comprises official SAP Signavio Process Navigator content, SAP Help Portal documentation, SAP Best Practices setup/test material, and SAP Fiori Apps Reference Library records; official method/learning sources are used where necessary. Each item receives an identifier, edition/release, URL, retrieval date, source grade, archive locator, and inclusion rationale. Rolling documentation is captured and qualified. Private-edition and on-premise sources are excluded from the main evidence unless explicitly analysed as contrasts.

## Analysis

First-stage descriptive coding records roles, user/system actions, objects, handoffs, workflows, decisions, approvals, rules, thresholds, exceptions, escalation, structural units, controls, statuses, access, master data, and configuration. Second-stage coding applies interdependence, coordination, information-processing, governance, and discretion constructs. The primitive designation requires a documented operation and organisational relation.

Analysis is relational. Interdependence requires differentiated activities and a specified relation; rights require actor, action/object, and scope; controls seek standard, comparison, and consequence. Each consequential interpretation includes a rival explanation and confidence judgement. Within-process maps reconstruct normal and exception configurations. Cross-process matrices compare mechanisms rather than raw feature counts. Macro claims require replication across domains and structural evidence.

The study analyses encoded organisational logic. Reference roles are not treated as jobs, capabilities as configurations, or process models as practice. Vendor bias, documentary incompleteness, release drift, theory forcing, and SAP specificity delimit claims. The research cannot infer implementation, user behaviour, coordination performance, or organisational outcomes.
