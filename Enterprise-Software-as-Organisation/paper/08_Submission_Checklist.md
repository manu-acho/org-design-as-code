# Submission Checklist

- [ ] One-sentence contribution is specific, evidenced, and non-hyperbolic.
- [ ] Research question, theory, unit, data, analysis, and claims align.
- [ ] Every empirical claim links to evidence IDs and a source-grade check.
- [ ] Corpus release, dates, IDs, size, exclusions, and missingness are reported.
- [ ] Encoded/implemented/enacted distinction appears in introduction, method, findings, discussion, and conclusion.
- [ ] Negative cases and rival explanations materially affect claims.
- [ ] No capability is described as implemented; no architecture is described as outcome.
- [ ] Primitive inclusion/exclusion criteria are applied consistently.
- [ ] Tables/figures have provenance and do not reproduce protected content improperly.
- [ ] All citations exist in `.bib`; metadata and quoted text are verified.
- [ ] Ethics, licence, copyright, and data-access requirements are addressed.
- [ ] Audit materials and reproducibility statement are prepared for permissible sharing.
- [ ] Journal aims, word limit, style, anonymisation, data policy, and AI policy are checked at submission date.
- [ ] Title, abstract, keywords, highlights, author details, acknowledgements, conflicts, funding, and cover letter are complete.
- [ ] A cold reader can state the contribution and its boundary after one reading.
