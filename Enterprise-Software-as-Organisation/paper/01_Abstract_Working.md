# Working Abstracts

## Information Systems framing

Enterprise-systems research has largely examined technologies as they are selected, implemented, adapted, and used. This study shifts attention to the enterprise-software reference architecture as an empirical object. It asks how organisational coordination and governance mechanisms are encoded within SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition. The study concerns encoded logic, not implemented configuration or enacted practice.

## Organisation Theory framing

Organisation theory explains how differentiated activities are coordinated, informed, and governed, but typically treats the organisation rather than software architecture as the locus of design. This study asks what model of organisation can be reconstructed from an enterprise-software reference architecture. ## Business Transformation framing

Fit-to-Standard implementation begins from vendor reference processes, yet the organisational content of those standards is often left implicit.
