## Evidence convention

Statements in these guides are classified implicitly by source type:

1. **Official SAP material** is the authority for current product, method, and reference-process descriptions.
2. **Supplied study notes** are a learning record and discovery aid. They are not automatically authoritative or suitable for research citation.
3. **HackMD notes** are supplementary personal notes. At this review date, the available browser reached a sign-in shell rather than the note contents. Their links are preserved in the source register, but no claim in these guides depends uniquely on unread HackMD content.
4. **Research interpretation** is clearly framed as interpretation and must be tested against registered SAP artefacts before becoming a finding.

## Maintenance approach

Add each new block of course notes to the relevant lesson rather than creating disconnected summaries. For every process, preserve the same learning sequence:

1. business purpose and outcome;
2. organisational units and roles;
3. master data and business objects;
4. normal document and value flow;
5. accounting and cross-process integration;
6. controls, approvals, exceptions, and reporting;
7. configuration or extension points;
8. certification distinctions;
9. research observations and candidate evidence sources.

**Coverage boundary as of 15 September 2026:** the E2E guide covers foundations, enterprise structure and data, Record-to-Report, and the studied portion of Recruit-to-Retire. Detailed Source-to-Pay/P2P, Lead-to-Cash, Design-to-Operate, and Service content will be added as study progresses. The Activate guide covers the material currently present in the supplied notes, not every possible project-management examination topic.
