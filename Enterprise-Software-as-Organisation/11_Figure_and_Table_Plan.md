# Figure and Table Plan

## Figures in Paper 1

Paper 1 has six figures in the working manuscript, numbered in reading order. The first three establish the evidence boundary, theory and method. The next three are compact worked findings placed beside the passages they explain. All have editable SVG masters. Final figure selection, journal-specific sizing and export remain part of the submission audit.

## Concepts kept outside the main paper

- **Expanded layered model:** retained in the [conceptual framework](02_Conceptual_Framework.md) for theory development and teaching. Figure 2 presents the relations needed for the paper's argument, without reproducing the full model.
- **Detailed workflow and coding rules:** retained in the [analytical framework](09_Analytical_Framework.md). Figure 3 shows the steps and feedback needed to follow the manuscript's inference.
- **Continuous cross-case process flow:** not proposed for Paper 1. Figure 6 and Table 5 compare independently documented operations without implying that one transaction was traced across J45, BD9 and J58.
