# Springer Book Proposal  -  Working Skeleton

**Status:** Internal development; not ready for submission  
**Publisher:** Springer Nature, subject to editorial fit and proposal review  
**Working title:** *Enterprise Software as Organisation Design: Coordination, Processes and Transformation in SAP S/4HANA*

## 1. Brief description

This research monograph examines enterprise software as codified organisation design. Using release-controlled SAP S/4HANA reference artefacts, it reconstructs how end-to-end processes represent dependencies, responsibilities, decision points, information visibility, control, exception handling, and coordination. It develops an interdisciplinary framework connecting organisation design, coordination theory, decision theory, organisational routines, sociomateriality, and enterprise-systems research.

The book makes a careful distinction between what software architecture encodes, what adopting organisations configure, and what people enact. It therefore offers an alternative to both technological determinism and accounts that treat enterprise architecture as merely a backdrop to implementation. Comparative analyses of Procure to Pay, Order to Cash, Record to Report, and potentially Recruit to Retire demonstrate the method and support a theory of software organisational primitives.
