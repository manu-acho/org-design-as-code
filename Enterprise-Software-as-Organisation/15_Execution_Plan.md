# Twelve-Week Execution Plan

| Week | Objective and activities | Concrete deliverable | Completion criterion | Manuscript effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Freeze release/scope; verify theory bibliography; run pilot searches | Scope memo, source map, verified `.bib` v1 | Included process IDs and release recorded | Introduction/method boundary |
| 2 | Collect enterprise structure and cross-cutting IAM/configuration evidence | Registered structural/governance mini-corpus | Required metadata and captures complete | Case/method; context table |
| 3 | Collect P2P across all artefact classes | P2P corpus and completeness checklist | Normal, control, exception and discretion searches logged | Findings evidence base |
| 4 | Collect O2C | O2C corpus and checklist | Same standard as week 3 | Findings evidence base |
| 5 | Collect R2R | R2R corpus and checklist | Same standard as week 3 | Findings evidence base |
| 6 | Pilot two-stage coding; revise manual | Codebook v1.0, coding-decision log, pilot matrix | Ambiguities resolved or documented | Method coding section |
| 7 | Complete P2P coding and mechanism map | P2P within-process memo/display | Claim candidates trace to evidence and rivals | Findings section 1 |
| 8 | Complete O2C and R2R coding | Two within-process memos/displays | Negative cases searched | Findings sections 2–3 |
| 9 | Cross-process/multilevel comparison | Master matrix, primitive ledger, pattern memo | Each macro candidate meets replication rule | Findings synthesis |
| 10 | Adversarial analysis and theory refinement | Claim–evidence–rival table; rejection log | Every candidate has drop/narrow/retain decision | Discussion and limitations |
| 11 | Write complete manuscript and produce figures/tables | Full draft with no unsupported placeholders except declared items | All claims link to evidence IDs | Entire paper |
| 12 | Internal review, reference audit, journal adaptation | Submission-ready manuscript, supplements, cover-letter draft | Checklist passes; target requirements verified | Submission package |

The plan is artefact-driven. Reading is undertaken when it resolves a construct or positioning problem and must result in a literature-matrix entry or theory memo. If collection slips, narrow process coverage explicitly rather than fabricate completeness; preserve time for analysis and writing.
