# Module 2 reading pack: End-to-End Processes

| Role | Reading and access | Question to bring |
|---|---|---|
| Required orientation | [Student lesson](../modules/02_End_to_End_Processes/01_Student_Lesson.md). Original course draft. | What result defines the end of the process you are analysing? |
| Technical orientation | [SAP Learning: Exploring End-to-End Business Processes in SAP](https://learning.sap.com/courses/exploring-end-to-end-business-processes-in-sap). Official public course outline checked; use the domain names for orientation. No training completion is required. | How do SAP's broader process names differ from the bounded case windows? |
| Essential theory | [Van der Aalst (2013), *Business Process Management: A Comprehensive Survey*](https://doi.org/10.1155/2013/507984). Publisher text checked; read the abstract and the discussion that distinguishes models and event logs. | What can an event log show that a reference model cannot? What can it miss? |
| Essential application | [J45 and BD9 dossier](../cases/M02_J45_and_BD9_Process_Reconstruction.md). Original summaries of release-controlled material; exact locators and web-access limitations recorded. | Which arrows are supported, and where should the map stop? |
| Optional modelling | [Mendling, Reijers and van der Aalst (2010), *Seven Process Modeling Guidelines*](https://www.vdaalst.com/publications/p574.pdf). Original article at an author's site; the link provides the full text. | What makes a model understandable without sacrificing important branches? |
| Optional broader reading | [Dumas, La Rosa, Mendling and Reijers (2018), *Fundamentals of Business Process Management*, second edition](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-662-56509-4). Publisher record checked; chapter access may depend on a library. | How would the model be used differently for discovery, redesign or monitoring? |

The [Stream 9 review](../../literature/Stream_09_Business_Process_Management_Process_Architecture_Detailed_Review.md) and [shared bibliography](../../references/references.bib) provide the deeper research route. The lesson and dossier are enough for the exercise. No proprietary source material is reproduced.
