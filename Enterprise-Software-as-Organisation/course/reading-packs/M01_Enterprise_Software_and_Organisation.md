# Module 1 reading pack: Enterprise Software and Organisation

No SAP training or prior enterprise architecture study is required. Read the orientation and case first. The scholarly readings explain why the course distinguishes the technical arrangement from work in use.

| Role | Reading and access | Question to bring |
|---|---|---|
| Required orientation | [Student lesson](../modules/01_Enterprise_Software_and_Organisation/01_Student_Lesson.md). Original course draft. | How do the two grocery designs allocate decisions differently? |
| Short technical orientation | [SAP: What is ERP?](https://www.sap.com/resources/what-is-erp). Public vendor overview checked. Read the definition; treat benefits as vendor claims. | What business functions may share data or depend on one another? |
| Essential enterprise-system reading | [Davenport (1998), *Putting the Enterprise into the Enterprise System*](https://hbr.org/1998/07/putting-the-enterprise-into-the-enterprise-system). Read the public introduction and, if available, the discussion of package logic. Publisher introduction and product summary checked. | Why is integration also a question about how work is organised? |
| Essential practice reading | [Feldman and Pentland (2003), *Reconceptualizing Organizational Routines*](https://doi.org/10.2307/3556620), abstract. Publisher abstract checked; a university-hosted copy is available. | How does a general pattern differ from a particular performance? |
| Required application | [BD9 evidence cards](../cases/M01_BD9_Reading_a_Reference_Artefact.md). Original summaries from the registered Swiss evidence, with exact locators and access limitations. | What is documented as an input, and what is only an expected result? |
| Optional extensions | [Orlikowski (2000)](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.11.4.404.14600) and [Hanseth and Lyytinen (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1057/jit.2009.19), abstracts checked. | How does study of situated use differ from study of an evolving infrastructure? |

Use the [shared bibliography](../../references/references.bib) and the reviews of [enterprise systems](../../literature/Stream_04_Enterprise_Systems_Implementation_Transformation_Detailed_Review.md), [routines](../../literature/Stream_05_Organisational_Routines_Detailed_Review.md), [materiality](../../literature/Stream_06_Technology_Materiality_Organisation_Detailed_Review.md) and [infrastructure](../../literature/Stream_07_Digital_Infrastructure_Standards_Classification_Detailed_Review.md) if you want to read further. If an article or original procedure is unavailable, the lesson and cards are sufficient for the seminar. Record what you actually read.
