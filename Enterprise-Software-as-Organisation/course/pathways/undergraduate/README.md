# Undergraduate Pathway

## Provisional position

An advanced undergraduate course for information systems, business administration, operations, accounting, or business-process management students.

## Adaptation principles

- retain the encoded, implemented, and enacted distinction;
- retain organisation design, coordination, information, and decision foundations;
- use fewer required original research articles;
- provide vocabulary support and worked examples;
- assess recognition and differentiation before independent theoretical evaluation;
- use narrower, strongly scaffolded documentary cases;
- retain all source and inference requirements.

## Development gate

Detailed adaptation begins only after the relevant postgraduate modules have complete student lessons, activities, and verified cases.
