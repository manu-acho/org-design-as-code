# Executive Pathway

## Provisional position

A short programme for transformation leaders, process owners, enterprise architects, SAP programme managers, senior consultants, and governance professionals.

## Adaptation principles

- organise sessions around transformation decisions and diagnostic questions;
- retain the theoretical basis but reduce literature-review activity;
- use verified process cases and workshop artefacts;
- focus on standardisation, local variation, discretion, exception design, and governance;
- require participants to distinguish architectural claims from implementation evidence;
- end with an organisational design or evidence action plan.

## Possible formats

- three-day intensive programme;
- five half-day workshops;
- executive certificate with applied organisational project.

## Development gate

The pathway should not be marketed or taught until at least one complete process case and the associated master modules are teaching-ready.
