# Exercise EO-EX-01: Make one claim you can defend

**Module 1 · Week 1 · Formative introduction**

Read the [lesson](../modules/01_Enterprise_Software_and_Organisation/01_Student_Lesson.md) and bring the [two evidence cards](../cases/M01_BD9_Reading_a_Reference_Artefact.md). Use the cards even if you cannot open the original pages. If you read only the cards, label your source as a mediated summary. No SAP login is required.

1. **Read Card A.** Underline the actor, action, earlier record and expected result. Cite its evidence ID and steps. What does the earlier record do?
2. **Compare Card B.** Say what passes to the later activity. Is it a selection input, a value used downstream, or only a visible label? Explain your classification using the card rather than the screen or process name.
3. **Test the claim.** A colleague says, “Once a delivery number exists, the customer has received the goods.” Write a supported replacement and state what evidence would be needed to assess the colleague's claim.
4. **Sort the levels.** Classify the following fictional examples: a reference instruction, a retailer's tested configuration, a record of staff discussing and handling a real shortage, and a comparison of wrong-item deliveries before and after a change. For each, state one conclusion it can support and one it cannot. Identify one further check before attributing a difference to the change.

**Time in class:** 25 minutes for tasks 1 to 3. Use task 4 as a short follow-up or as preparation for the seminar's evidence-level discussion. **After class, submit:** A two-row evidence table and 100 to 160 words explaining one claim, one rival and one boundary. A diagram is optional; include a text equivalent if you use one.

| What we will look for | What a strong answer does |
|---|---|
| Accurate reading | Names a locatable operation and preserves the optional branch and expected state. |
| Useful interpretation | Explains which task depends on what instead of saying the system “integrates everything”. |
| Restraint | Separates represented work, customer setup, what people did and a measured outcome. |

This exercise rehearses outcomes 1, 2, 3 and 12 for the [weekly analytical portfolio](../04_Assessment_Strategy.md#weekly-analytical-portfolio). It does not add a graded component.
