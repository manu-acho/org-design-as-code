# Exercise IP-EX-01: Trace a validation result into a decision

**Module 5 · Postgraduate · Formative portfolio practice**

**Preparation:** Read [the lesson](../modules/05_Information_Processing/01_Student_Lesson.md) and the [case cards and fictional brief](../cases/M05_J58_Balance_Validation.md). **In class:** 35 minutes. No SAP account or TOGAF preparation is required.

1. **Trace the documentary path.** Write one row for the scheduled run and one for results inspection. For each, identify the input or state, what the procedure represents, and a claim you cannot make. Include the evidence ID and step range. Mark unknowns rather than filling them in.
2. **Diagnose a missing result.** Compare three explanations: the rule was outside the run scope, the initial view hid a success, or the viewer was not authorised to see the result. Give a check that would discriminate each from the others. Mark the third as a possibility to test, not a finding about J58.
3. **Compare two design responses for the fictional company.** Choose one that reduces a named information requirement or reduces referrals to the close lead, and one that increases capacity to resolve the remaining cases. Say what each costs and where the remaining work sits.
4. **Write a small architecture gap and test.** Contrast the fictional baseline and target, name the gap, and propose one test with an expected result. Include a negative access check. List one capability or ownership decision the implementation team still needs to verify. Do not treat the target as a delivered SAP design.

**In class:** Sketch the two documentary rows and one test. **After class, submit:** The map or table with a text equivalent, a baseline-target-gap row, and 180 to 250 words of reasoning. Use labels for documentary observations, fictional assumptions and proposals. A reference link is not a substitute for stating what evidence you actually used.

| Criterion | A strong answer |
|---|---|
| Traceability | Distinguishes job scope from the results view and gives a locator for each. |
| Rival reasoning | Uses a different check for each possible cause and preserves uncertainty. |
| Organisation design | Compares real alternatives and explains who takes on the work or has to act. |
| Architecture and verification | Connects a proposed target to a client requirement and tests visibility separately from permission and sign-off. |

This activity rehearses programme outcomes 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 11 and 12 for the [weekly analytical portfolio](../04_Assessment_Strategy.md#weekly-analytical-portfolio). It does not add a graded component.
