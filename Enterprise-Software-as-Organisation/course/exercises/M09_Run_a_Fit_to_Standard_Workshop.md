# Workshop FTS-EX-01: Run a Fit-to-Standard session and hand it to delivery

**Module 9 · Postgraduate · Week 11 · Formative consulting studio**

**Before class:** Read the [lesson](../modules/09_SAP_Activate_and_Transformation/01_Student_Lesson.md). Allow a further 30 minutes to read the [case cards and client brief](../cases/M09_J45_Fit_to_Standard.md), check the scope and write one question for a process owner and one for a technical lead. No live SAP or TOGAF certification is required.

## In class: 55-minute simulation

| Time | Activity | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 min | Open the workshop. State the purpose, process boundary and who can decide. | Scope and one downstream dependency. |
| 5 to 15 min | Walk through N1 and the relevant reference cards. Introduce N2 and the optional route. | Reference observations with evidence IDs; assumptions about the client kept separate. |
| 15 to 30 min | Interview the client roles. Ask what a block means, who can release it, and whether the email preference conceals a control or evidence need. | A fit decision log for FTS-01 to FTS-05. Mark genuine uncertainty. |
| 30 to 45 min | Decide what can proceed and what needs investigation. Build the handoff for FTS-02 and one related item. | A baseline-target-gap row and two prioritised backlog entries. |
| 45 to 55 min | Read the decisions back to stakeholders. Present one positive and one negative test for FTS-02. | Owner, expected state, required evidence and one unresolved dependency. |

Use this **course template**, not an SAP form:

| Requirement ID | Reference observation and locator | Client need and reason | Candidate response | Decision status | Owner and next evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FTS-__ | What was shown? | What is required? | Standard, configuration, process change, feasibility, extension, integration or defer. | Proposed, agreed in the simulation, or open. | Who checks what before committing? |

**A backlog entry** needs an ID, an owner, priority and rationale, a dependency or open decision, and an acceptance condition. For a feasibility item, the acceptance condition may be a documented capability test and a decision, not delivered functionality.

## After class: delivery and evaluation portfolio

1. Complete the log and backlog. For FTS-02, compare standard-based enforcement with one other response. State what would disqualify either option. Do not invent a setting or released interface.
2. Turn the workshop test sketches into a matrix covering routine handling, independent release, self-release refusal, a legitimate no-PO case and an attempted alternate-route bypass. Name the test identity, setup, action, expected state and evidence. If the expected state is not agreed, record an open policy decision.
3. Add a cutover rehearsal for N5 and one operational review measure. Specify an owner and the observation that would tell you whether people actually follow the intended route. Explain why a successful test does not establish operational improvement.

**Submit:** The decision log, two backlog entries, a test matrix and a 250 to 350 word handoff note. Mark which facts came from SAP reference evidence and which belong only to the simulation.

| Feedback criterion | A strong submission |
|---|---|
| Evidence and scope | Uses the exact J45 locators and does not mistake a reference step for an implementation outcome. |
| Fit judgement | Separates business need, evidence, options and approval. Distinguishes a stated preference from a verified control need. |
| Delivery traceability | Preserves requirement IDs, owners, expected states, negative checks and dependencies. |
| Organisation design | Explains how the decision changes roles, evidence or authority and how it can be investigated in use. |

This activity rehearses outcomes 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11 and 12 for the [weekly analytical portfolio](../04_Assessment_Strategy.md#weekly-analytical-portfolio).
