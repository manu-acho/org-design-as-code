# Exercise KB-EX-01: Translate the record before recommending a change

**Module 6 · Postgraduate · Formative portfolio practice**

**Preparation:** Read the [lesson](../modules/06_Knowledge_Boundaries/01_Student_Lesson.md) and the [documentary card and fictional client brief](../cases/M06_J45_One_Order_Different_Meanings.md). **In class:** 35 minutes. No SAP system is required.

1. **Trace the documented handoff.** Identify what the earlier purchase order supplies to invoice entry and cite the evidence ID and locator. Explain what item population shows that mere visibility would not. State one claim that remains unsupported.
2. **Represent the same order line from different functions.** Assign Purchasing, Receiving, Quality and Accounts Payable in small groups, or use written role prompts. For each, record the event, quantity or state it can speak to, the evidence it needs and what it cannot decide. Ask one participant to restate another role's meaning in their own words.
3. **Diagnose three boundaries.** Give one potential transfer failure, one actual difference of meaning in the scenario or a clearly labelled example, and one potential clash of interests. Propose a response for each. For the pragmatic problem, compare two policies and name who must be consulted or given the decision.
4. **Record one design gap and a test.** Trace what Quality must receive and what Purchasing or Accounts Payable must get back. Write a positive check with a scoped finding and a negative check with inspection still pending. In one check, have the recipient explain what the finding means for their own work. Mark what would require product or configuration verification.

**In class:** Build the cross-role map, practise teach-back and sketch the checks. **After class, submit:** One cross-role table or diagram with a text equivalent, plus 180 to 250 words of reasoning. Separate documented observations, fictional facts and proposals. Include a sentence identifying what you would need to observe before calling the record a boundary object in practice.

| Criterion | A strong answer |
|---|---|
| Provenance | Cites J45-W07-16 and does not invent a documented inspection or payment path. |
| Boundary diagnosis | Explains why each proposed remedy fits the obstacle. |
| Cross-functional reasoning | Separates commercial commitment, physical count, quality acceptance and supplier claim without imputing decision rights. |
| Design and verification | Proposes an evidence-based handoff and a negative test rather than assuming product capability or a payable quantity. |

The exercise rehearses programme outcomes 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 11 and 12 for the [weekly analytical portfolio](../04_Assessment_Strategy.md#weekly-analytical-portfolio).
