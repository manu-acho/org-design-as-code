# Instructor Resources

Instructor material will support consistent teaching without prescribing a single interpretation where evidence permits alternatives.

Each module's instructor notes should include:

- the teaching purpose and place in the cumulative argument;
- assumed prior knowledge;
- a timed session plan;
- explanations of difficult concepts;
- likely misconceptions;
- worked analytical routes;
- alternative defensible answers;
- claims the evidence cannot support;
- links to cases, exercises, readings, and assessments;
- accessibility and participation options.

Use [SESSION_PLAN_TEMPLATE.md](SESSION_PLAN_TEMPLATE.md) for individual sessions.
