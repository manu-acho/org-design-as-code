# Module 4: Coordination and Interdependence

## Status

**Stage:** Complete first draft package for Weeks 5 and 6. Teaching and access review pending.

**Teaching-ready:** No

## Organisational problem

Once work is divided, activities depend on one another. The module asks what those dependencies are and which mechanisms are intended to manage them.

## Learning outcomes

After working through this module, you can:

1. identify major dependency families from documentary evidence;
2. relate a dependency to a candidate coordination mechanism;
3. distinguish a mechanism from accountability, predictability, common understanding, and performance;
4. explain why formal mechanisms and situated adjustment may coexist;
5. formulate a rival interpretation of an apparent coordination feature.

## How this module fits

Bring the two invoice-route maps from [Module 3](../03_Organisation_Design/README.md). This module asks what the tasks in such a map need from one another and how arrangements help meet those needs. It prepares the question for Module 5: what makes information usable at a handoff? The [Stream 2 review](../../../literature/Stream_02_Coordination_Theory_Detailed_Review.md) gives the full theoretical argument; the [coding manual](../../../08_Coding_Manual.md) explains the research method behind the documentary case.

The dependency-mechanism-condition map rehearses the [weekly analytical portfolio](../../04_Assessment_Strategy.md#weekly-analytical-portfolio). No live SAP access is required. Before teaching, review reading access, diagram accessibility, evidence boundaries and session timing.
