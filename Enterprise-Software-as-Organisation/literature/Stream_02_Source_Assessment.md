# Stream Two: Source Assessment

## Audit method and access labels

The supplied LeapSpace report was treated as a candidate-source map. Publication identity was checked against DOI, publisher, author repository, or institutional repository records. Access labels mean:

- **Full text read:** substantive text was available and inspected.
- **Abstract read:** only the abstract and bibliographic record supported the synthesis.
- **Metadata verified:** identity was checked, but no substantive claim is attributed beyond title/topic.
- **Unresolved:** the report did not provide a stable scholarly citation.

“Verified” does not mean that every proposition in a paper is accepted, and the review does not upgrade abstract access into full-text evidence.

The report’s “High” confidence designation is rejected. Agreement among references is not meaningful when the set mixes unrelated levels of analysis, opaque uploads, the research prompt, technical engineering studies, and specialised applications.

## Important omissions repaired

The LeapSpace report omitted several sources necessary to connect mechanisms with organisational accomplishment and knowledge boundaries: Okhuysen and Bechky (2009), Bechky (2003), Carlile (2002, 2004), Kellogg, Orlikowski, and Yates (2006), Jarzabkowski, Lê, and Feldman (2012), Schmidt and Simone (1996), Crowston (1997), and Faraj and Sproull (2000). Their inclusion materially changes the review: software artefacts can no longer be treated as self-executing coordination mechanisms or shared data as common understanding.

## Corrections to the supplied report

1. “Coordination artefact” is useful but should not become a residual label for every screen, record, or technical object.
2. Accountability, predictability, and common understanding are potential integrative conditions, not properties directly visible in configuration documentation.
3. Governance is not simply absent from coordination theory; decision rights and legitimate authority nevertheless require a distinct analytical lens.
4. Evidence from agile development teams, healthcare, supply chains, multi-agent systems, or interoperability cannot establish how SAP reference processes organise adopting firms.
5. Publication counts, citation convergence, and a tool-generated confidence label do not replace source relevance, construct validity, or full-text review.
